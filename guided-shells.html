The sampling clocks

Both paths use the same initial state, guidance coefficient λ, and update budget K over the same time interval. Increasing α reduces K and enlarges each time step. The guidance coefficient λ remains unchanged.

The noise-conditioned prior follows a probability-flow Euler step tied to the decrease in variance. Without guidance, its update is Δx = ½(σk2 − σk+12) s(xk, σk). Its motion and shrinking training band therefore advance on the same noise clock.

The state-only field uses position-dependent scaling and receives no scheduled noise label. Its implicit scale follows the current position; it need not wait for the blue schedule to reach its final value.

Guided updates retain both the prior and task gradient, with positive diagonal damping. Outside the scheduled training band, a smooth illustrative error field varies with the position and assigned label. It depicts possible learned-score error, not measured network behavior or an undefined analytic Gaussian score. Playback speed changes viewing time only.
